# Moving Bibic Winery to the new app: what came across and what didn't

*Prepared from the copy of your live data taken on 3 October 2026. This is the plain-language version of the technical report (document 18), which is kept for reference.*

## The short version

Everything that runs your day-to-day business has been copied into the new app, and it **matches the old system to the cent**: your customers, products and stock, orders, suppliers, costs, money received, the cellar and the vineyards.

To avoid dropping anything meaningful, we also added four things to the new app: customer categories (Restaurant, Hotel and so on), a "Cancelled" status for work orders, VAT on money-received entries, and room for longer cost descriptions.

What was **not** copied are the areas the new app doesn't cover yet: the till sales records, hospitality and reservations, staff scheduling, the kitchen, the wine club, the sales pipeline, bank statement lines and e-invoice files. Nothing has been deleted from the old system, so all of that is still there if you need it.

Two things need a decision or action from you before the team relies on the new app, and who can do what now matches the old app. They are explained in "What needs your attention" at the end:

1. **Several staff roles do work that is not in the new app yet**, so a few people will find little to do there for now.
2. **Costs and money received** have no screens in the new app yet, so that day-to-day work still happens in the old system.

## How we know it is correct

After copying, we ran **76 separate comparisons** between the old system and the new one: record counts and money totals for every area. **All 76 match, with no differences.** The headline figures above (order value, costs, money received, amount owed) are taken from those comparisons.

Copying the data again would not create duplicates or change anything. It was also tried first as a rehearsal that changed nothing, before the real run.

## What needs your attention

### 1. Who can do what now matches the old app

Each person kept their roles, and each role now allows what it allowed in the old app. Here is what that means for your team:

| Person | Roles | Can do in the new app |
|---|---|---|
| Filip, Karla | Administrator | Everything |
| Dragan | Cellar, Orders, Inventory | Orders, create customers, view inventory and record stock movements, the cellar and vineyards, work orders, see money figures |
| Ramon | Cellar, Orders | Orders, create customers, the cellar and vineyards, work orders, see money figures |
| Bojan | Manager, Employee, Orders | Orders, create customers, see money figures. No work orders (see below) |
| Slavica | Orders, Hospitality, Inventory | Orders, create customers, view inventory and record stock movements, work orders, see money figures |
| Nikola | Sales, Employee | See money figures only (the sales pipeline is not in the new app yet) |
| Vesna | Manager | See money figures only |
| Dražen | Hospitality | Nothing beyond the dashboard (hospitality is not in the new app yet) |

A few things worth knowing, all copied from how the old app behaved:

- **Only administrators** handle costs, money received, cash flow, suppliers, purchase-order changes, prices, customer editing, settings and the team list.
- **Creating and editing products** is for the Team role only. Inventory staff can see products and record stock movements, but not create or edit them.
- **Work orders** are for Team, Cellar and Orders. Anyone who also holds the Manager role never sees them, even if they hold Orders as well. That is why Bojan has none. Administrators always see everything.
- **Customers:** the customer list and customer pages are for administrators. Team and Orders staff can create customers and use the reorder reminders.
- **The dashboard** shows each person only the parts they could see elsewhere. Money and sales figures need the "see money figures" permission. Costs, cash flow, salaries and runway are administrator-only. Order counts need orders, low stock needs inventory, reorder reminders follow the customer rules, and tasks follow work orders. Someone with none of these (for example the Hospitality role) sees a short welcome note instead. The restricted figures are not sent to their screen at all, not just hidden.

Four judgement calls, where the old app was not entirely consistent:

1. In the old app, some behind-the-scenes functions would have let any signed-in person read the customer list, even though the menu only showed it to administrators. We followed what people actually saw: menu and pages.
2. The old app let Sales staff add a customer from inside an order form. Sales can't create orders, so we left that out.
3. The new AI data entry feature has no equivalent in the old app. Because it creates costs, money received entries and suppliers, which only administrators could handle, we made it administrator-only too.
4. Cellar costs are money figures, and were administrator-only in the old app, so they are here too.

This also means the new app can no longer be used by Manager, Sales, Hospitality, Kitchen, Employee or Wine Club roles for much, until the areas those roles worked in (people management, the sales pipeline, hospitality, the kitchen, the wine club) are built.

### 2. Costs and money received have no screen yet

The records are all in the new app, but the pages for browsing and entering costs and money received are not built yet (they appear in the menu as placeholders). Until they are, staff can't add or review these from the new app's screens, so the old system is still where that daily work happens.

One VAT difference to know about: the old app filled in VAT by itself, by reading e-invoices and by scanning customer invoices. The new app does not do that yet, so for new entries VAT has to be typed in.

### 3. Staff who need the missing areas

Two people mainly use areas that aren't in the new app yet:

- **Dražen** (reservations) has almost nothing to do in the new app until hospitality is built.
- **Nikola** (sales) can only see financial figures until the sales pipeline is built.

Slavica also has a hospitality role that has no effect yet.

### 4. A few things to tidy up or double-check

- **1 customer** (Restavracija Hotela Marina - Izola) needs a real email address (see above).
- **8 products** have a stock count that isn't explained by the recorded stock movements. This is normal when stock was counted at the start, but please spot-check them.
- **2 vessels** (ST23 and S01) show more wine than their capacity. That is how the old system had them, so it may just be a data-entry error to correct.
- **1 vessel and 9 wine lots** have a recorded volume that doesn't match what is inside them. Please check these in the cellar, as some may simply be emptied or bottled lots.

### 5. Try it with real work

The numbers all match, but we have not yet had someone walk through the screens with the imported data. We suggest a short session with the team using real orders and the cellar before relying on it.

### 6. Keep the old system for now

We recommend keeping the old system available (read-only) until the missing areas have been built and you are happy with the new app. It holds the till sales, reservations and staff records that were not copied.
