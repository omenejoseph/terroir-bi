An **order link** is a private web address a customer uses to place orders themselves — no login needed. They see your for-sale wines at their own prices.

## Create one

1. Open the customer under **Customers**.
2. Click **Generate Order Link**, then **Generate link**.
3. Copy the link and send it to the customer.

Anyone with the link can order for that customer, so send it only to them. If it leaks, use **Revoke order link** and generate a new one.

## Options

On the customer record, under **Advanced settings**:

- **Hide prices on order link** — they order without seeing prices.
- **Allow single bottle orders** — they may order single bottles instead of whole cases.

Orders placed through the link appear under **Orders** like any other, and you are notified.
