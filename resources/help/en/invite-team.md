Everyone should have their own login — it keeps the audit log meaningful and lets you give each person only the access they need.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Team** in the sidebar (under *System*).
2. Click **Invite**, enter their email and pick their roles.
3. Click **Create invite link** and send the link to them yourself (by email or chat). It works once and expires.

Until they accept, they are listed under **Pending invitations**, where you can cancel the invitation.

## Roles

A person can hold more than one role:

- **Admin** — everything, including settings, the team and deleting records.
- **Team** — orders, inventory and production; can add new customers.
- **Orders** — orders; can add new customers.
- **Cellar** — the cellar and vineyards: vessels, lots, additions, analyses, bottling.
- **Manager** and **Sales** — can see the financial figures.

Only admins see the customer list, settings and the team page.

Change someone's roles later with **Manage roles** on their row. Two extra switches control whether they may edit orders and see shipped orders.
