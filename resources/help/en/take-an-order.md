## Create the order

1. On **Orders** (or the **Dashboard**), click **New order**.
2. Pick the **Customer**. Their prices — tier, rebate or agreed prices — are filled in for you.
3. Under **Products**, add the wines and quantities. Each line shows whether it is in bottles or cases.
4. Optionally add **Notes**, a **Shipping cost** and who pays logistics (**We pay logistics** or **Customer pays logistics**).
5. Click **Place order**.

The order starts as **Received** and its stock is taken off the shelf straight away.

## Order settings

Open **Order settings** before placing the order for the special kinds:

- **Backorder** — you don't have the stock yet; it isn't deducted until you choose to.
- **Consignment** — the goods stay yours until the customer sells them.
- **Allocation** — sets stock aside for this customer. It stays in the cellar and only leaves when the order ships.

## Customer not there yet?

Click **New customer** inside the order panel to add them without leaving the order.
