## Add a customer

1. Open **Customers** and click **New customer**.
2. Enter their **OIB / VAT** number and click **Look up** — for Croatian OIBs and EU VAT numbers the name and address are filled in for you.
3. Check the **Company name**, **Contact name**, **Email** and **Phone**.
4. Under **Pricing**, pick their **Pricing tier**, or enter a **Rebate %** of their own.
5. Optionally pick a **Category** (restaurant, shop, distributor…).
6. Click **Create customer**.

## Pricing in one sentence

A customer pays the list price, minus their tier's rebate — or their own rebate, if they have one, which replaces the tier's. Agreed per-product prices override both. See *How prices are worked out*.

## Advanced settings

- **Exclude from statistics** — for internal or test customers that shouldn't count in reports.
- **Hide prices on order link** and **Allow single bottle orders** — how their self-service order link behaves.
