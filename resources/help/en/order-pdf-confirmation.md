## Print or download

Open the order and click **Print**. The PDF shows your company details as the sender, the customer's details, every line with its price, and the total excluding VAT.

The sender block comes from **Settings** — company name, tax ID, address, contact and, at the bottom, your IBAN. If something is missing on the PDF, fill it in there.

## Confirmation email

When an order is placed, the customer can receive a confirmation by email. To send it again — for example after changing the order — open it and click **Resend confirmation**.

The email goes to the address on the customer's record, so keep their **Email** up to date under **Customers**.
