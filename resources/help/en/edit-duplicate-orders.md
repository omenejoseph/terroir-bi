Open the order from the **Orders** list.

## Change the lines

- Click **Add item** to add a product.
- Click a line to change its quantity or price, or remove it.

Stock follows every change: adding or increasing a line takes more stock, removing one puts it back.

## Repeat an order

Customers often re-order the same wines. Open their last order, then **More actions** → **Duplicate order**. A new order is created with the same customer and lines; check the quantities and save.

## Mark it paid

**Mark paid** records the full outstanding balance as paid. Partly paid orders show as such in the list.

## Delete

**More actions** → **Delete order** removes it and puts its stock back on the shelf. This can't be undone.
