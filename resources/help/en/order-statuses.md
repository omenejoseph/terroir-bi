Every order moves through four steps:

1. **Received** — the order is in.
2. **In Process** — someone is picking and packing it.
3. **Ready to Ship** — packed and waiting for transport.
4. **Shipped** — it has left.

Open an order and click the next step at the top (**Move to …**). On the order list you can select several orders and use **Bulk actions** to move them together.

## When stock moves

- A normal order takes its stock **when it is placed**.
- A **backorder** doesn't take stock until you decide it should.
- An **allocation** keeps the stock aside in the cellar and takes it **when the order ships**. If the stock isn't there, the order can't be marked shipped.
- **Deleting** an order puts its stock back on the shelf.

## Revenue

Whether the dashboard counts an order's revenue when it ships or when it is created is set in **Settings** → **Revenue target** → **Count revenue**.
