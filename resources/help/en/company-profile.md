Your company details are printed as the sender on every order PDF, and the bank details tell customers where to pay.

## Fill them in

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. On the **Organisation** tab, check the **Company name**, **Language** and **Timezone**, and enter your **Tax ID** (OIB).
3. Under **Address & contact**, enter the street address, postal code, city, country, and a contact email and phone.
4. Under **Bank details**, enter your **IBAN** and **Bank**.
5. Click **Save changes**.

The setup checklist's *Company profile* step is done once the tax ID, address, city, country and contact email are filled in. Without an IBAN it still counts, but warns you that PDFs go out without payment details.

## Currency

The currency is fixed when the account is created and can't be changed in Settings — changing it would relabel amounts already stored rather than convert them. Contact support if it is wrong.

## When revenue counts

On the **Revenue target** tab, **Count revenue** decides whether the dashboard counts an order when it *ships* or when it is *created*.
