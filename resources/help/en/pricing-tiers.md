A **pricing tier** is a price book for a group of customers — for example *Wholesale*, *Retail* or *Distributor*. Each tier has a default rebate taken off your list prices.

## Create a tier

1. Open **Customers** → **Pricing tiers**.
2. Click **New tier**, give it a name and a **Rebate %** (0 if customers on it pay list price).
3. Save.

Then put customers on it from their customer record (**Pricing tier**).

## Check the result

Below the tiers, the **Price preview** shows a few of your wines with their list price and what a customer on each tier pays. It uses exactly the same calculation as real orders.

## Fixed prices per tier

When a tier should pay a set price for one wine rather than "list minus rebate", open the product in **Inventory**, go to its **Pricing** tab and click **Add tier price**. The tier's rebate is then applied to that price instead of the list price.
