Can't find the answer here? Send a request to our support team.

1. Click **Help & support** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. At the bottom of the Help page, click **Contact support**.
3. Pick what it is about, write a short subject, and describe what you were trying to do and what happened instead.
4. Click **Send to support**.

We reply inside the app — you'll get a notification — and by email.

## Following up

Your requests are listed under **Your support requests** at the top of the Help page. Open one to read the conversation and reply. When it is sorted, click **Mark as resolved**. Replying to a resolved request reopens it.

Organisation admins can see every request their colleagues have sent; everyone else sees their own.
