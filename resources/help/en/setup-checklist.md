Open **Setup** in the sidebar (under *System*) to see everything your account still needs.

## How it works

The checklist reads your real data. **Finished products** turns green as soon as you have a wine that is for sale and has a list price — there is nothing to tick off by hand. If the data later goes away, the step goes back to open.

Each step has a button that opens the screen where the work happens. A strip at the top of that screen shows which step you are on, with **Back to setup** and **Next** buttons.

## The three sections

- **Go live** — what you need before your first order: company profile, product categories, finished products, opening stock, pricing tiers, customers and your team. The dashboard card counts only these.
- **Sell better** — product costs, special prices, customer categories, order links, reorder points and materials.
- **Go further** — recipes, photos, revenue targets and, if your plan includes them, the cellar and work orders.

## Skipping a step

Steps you might not need — pricing tiers, inviting a team, and everything in *Go further* — have a **Skip** button. **Opening stock** can be marked done by hand, because some wines are genuinely sold out.

Hide the dashboard reminder with **Hide the reminder from the dashboard** at the bottom of the Setup page. The page itself stays under *System · Setup*.
